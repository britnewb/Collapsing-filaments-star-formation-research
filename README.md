# Collapsing molecular cloud filaments in the context of star formation

My PhD thesis work on the collapsing of molecular cloud filaments leading to star formation.

The PhD thesis work took place from 2013-2015 at the University of Toronto under the supervision of Professor Christopher Matzner. The these was never published.


## Scientific background: 

Star formation happens preferrentially in molecular clouds, typically forming clusters of stars, rather than solitary stars. This behaviour is so pervasive that it has it's own rule, called the Initial Mass Function (IMF), which is a major open question in Astrophysics: Why do statrs form with relatively predictable masses and multiplicities?

In Physics, anything which happens in a predictable fashion must have a reason; this is our motivation for studying the IMF.
